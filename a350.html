Overview

Fingerprint, with its high contrast accuracy and low intrusion biometrics, is widely used in the market due to its convenience and easy acceptance. And it will keep developing deeply in time and attendance applications. The A350 series utilizes the latest fingerprint algorithms of Anviz with leading performance. It allows users to access the device anytime and anywhere via mobile terminals, providing a more flexible and smooth time attendance experience.
