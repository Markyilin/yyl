Overview

Servicing many businesses and purposes in the small and medium enterprise workplace, the W-Series is a perfect choice for a business's operational focus, including tracking employee attendance and managing their access. It offers seamless and consistent service as a cloud-based physical time attendance and access control solution.

 

Future-proof Convenience, You Can Do More

Simplify attendance access for your business with easy-to-deploy devices and cloud-based software designed for a safer, smarter workspace.

  • Enjoy High Performance in Multiple Biometrics

    Reduce your business's operational losses by targeting each employee's identity through accurate face and fingerprint recognition.

  • Cloud Manage Your Office Operations

    Configure devices and permissions from nearly anywhere at any time, and get insights at a glance to manage your organization.

  • Easy to Deploy Anywhere You Need

    Reduces on-site infrastructure to maintain, and simplifies the intelligent management of your business office.

Manage at Scale and Get Insights at a Glance

W Series connects to the CrossChex Cloud platform, which helps integrate your physical access attendance management system with the cloud, providing real-time data that can be used to enhance the employee experience and improve your operation's efficiency.
