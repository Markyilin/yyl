Overview

As outdoor environments pose a greater risk of intrusion and vandalism, stronger access control solutions are needed to protect personnel safety and site resources. M Series is a local-based outdoor access control solution tailored for medium to large organizations that fit seamlessly into any environment. It tightly controls the flow of staff and protects your work site.

 

Unlock Flexible Access Experience, Depending on Your Needs

Future-proofing your organization with next-generation security technology and business protection.

  • Select the Modern Access Ways You Need

  • Reliable All-weather Operation

    Even in extreme weather, the IP65 dust & water proof design can accurately realize personnel access control.

  • Ward Off the External Threats You Fear

    Featuring a rugged IK10 vandal-resistant metal housing, it can withstand high-intensity impacts from any outdoor conditions.

  • Highly Adaptable to the Work Site
